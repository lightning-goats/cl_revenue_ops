# cl-revenue-ops v3.0.0

## Standalone revenue operations and tournament-proven profitability

Version 3.0.0 is the first release of the smaller, fully standalone
`cl-revenue-ops` product. It concentrates authority on local routing economics:
fee control, budget-constrained circular rebalancing, profitability analysis,
revenue reporting, and spend enforcement.

This is a major release with intentional compatibility breaks. Read the
upgrade section before restarting the plugin.

## Highlights

- Revenue Ops now uses local node evidence only. The retired
  cl-mycelium/cl-hive coordination path cannot influence decisions.
- CapacityPlanner, automatic channel opening and closing, planner
  defibrillation, Boltz, LN+, swaps, and withdrawals are no longer execution
  surfaces.
- Core Lightning v26.06.7 or newer is mandatory. The plugin refuses to load on
  an older, missing, or malformed reported version while leaving `lightningd`
  itself running.
- The fee controller gained bounded, evidence-backed acquisition and selective
  displacement behavior that protects profitable routes while competing for
  traffic it can acquire profitably.
- Strategic channel inventory now uses the true local channel balance rather
  than an execution-limited spendability field.
- Native rebalancing gained more accurate pricing, safer recovery, better route
  learning, and stronger post-settlement accounting.
- A reproducible Revenue Ops-versus-CLBOSS tournament, living scorecard, and
  strict exclusion ledger now cover fees, profit, rebalancing, liquidity,
  mixed CLN/LND routing, reliability, and safety.

## Breaking changes and removed authority

The following capabilities are removed rather than deprecated aliases:

- automatic channel open and close;
- CapacityPlanner and planner defibrillation;
- Boltz execution and swap scheduling;
- LN+ application, obligation, and channel-opening execution;
- expansion-treasury execution;
- peer-ban mutation surfaces; and
- all former hive/mycelium coordination behavior.

Historical database tables and rows remain readable for accounting and audit
purposes, but they cannot schedule or authorize new work. Generic `no_close`
metadata remains stored for manual or external workflows even though this
plugin has no close executor.

Removed RPCs return method-not-found. Removed CLN options are unknown options;
an old config that still contains them can prevent the v3 plugin from starting.
Use the
[liquidity executor decommission runbook](../operations/LIQUIDITY_EXECUTOR_DECOMMISSION_RUNBOOK.md)
and its config renderer before stopping a v2 runtime.

The retained action surface is deliberately narrow: fee changes, circular
rebalances, governed spend-ledger operations, analysis/wake actions, and policy
or runtime-config writes. The
[action RPC inventory](../audits/CL_REVENUE_OPS_ACTION_RPC_INVENTORY.md)
classifies all 37 registered methods.

## Important correctness and safety fixes

### True channel inventory on large channels

Strategic balance decisions previously used `spendable_msat`. That field is an
execution/routability limit, not the channel's true local inventory. On a large
LND-adjacent Polar channel, the 4,294,967,295-msat execution ceiling made a
16,777,215-sat channel appear to hold only about 4.29M local sats and could
suppress an entire profitable acquisition market.

Revenue Ops now prefers `our_amount_msat`, then `to_us_msat`, for strategic
inventory. `spendable_msat` remains a conservative fallback and continues to
serve execution-capacity decisions. Neutral, missing, malformed, and RPC-error
paths remain bounded and crash-free.

### Ambiguous and late rebalance settlement

- A payment that times out or loses its RPC transport after dispatch remains
  pending instead of being recorded as a definite failure.
- Its budget reservation remains held until `listsendpays` proves a terminal
  outcome, preventing a second payment from being sent on top of a live HTLC.
- Late settlements record their actual msat fee exactly once and release or
  settle the governed reservation consistently.
- Pending-settlement pagination, restart recovery, missing-row recovery, and
  stale-hold visibility were hardened.
- Duplicate emergency refills and concurrent destination refills are blocked.

### Fee, route, and accounting correctness

- Rebalance route fees and costs remain millisatoshi-native through persistence
  and are rounded only at documented reporting boundaries.
- CLN 26 askrene route-hop fields and `maxparts` are parsed and passed
  correctly.
- A selected route may be requoted once for a strictly cheaper route only when
  modeled reliability does not decline and the original economic ceiling still
  accepts it.
- Failed route segments are attributed with confidence proportional to the
  available evidence; missing attribution no longer becomes unjustified
  high-confidence blame.
- Exclusion retries and smaller-amount retries preserve the original effective
  fee-rate ceiling and never retry an ambiguous in-flight payment.
- Settled rebalances refresh channel liquidity and profitability evidence
  before the next decision.
- Dry-run fee proposals no longer contaminate broadcast state.
- Startup budget repair, missing spend history, malformed inputs, and unavailable
  profitability or wallet evidence remain fail-closed for spend.

## Fee-setting and profit improvements

- Bounded acquisition experiments are enabled by default for cold but credible
  demand lanes and stop at explicit evidence, inventory, opportunity-cost, and
  time limits.
- Stable acquisition exits can graduate into paid retention rather than being
  discarded immediately after the acquisition cap fills.
- Proven acquisition routes may price one ppm below a route-specific competitor
  floor, but only within a 50-ppm inventory-credit bound and only while the
  evidence remains valid.
- Ordinary profitable flow is valued after acquisition and protected from
  accidental zero-flow or depleted-inventory fee ratchets.
- Extreme channel inventory can widen the fee response without overriding the
  global safety rails.
- Fee and rebalance cycles wake sooner on material new evidence, while repeated
  stale or malformed evidence remains neutral.

The strategy optimizes net profit rather than raw forwarding count. Volume is
valuable only when its incremental fees exceed the liquidity and execution
cost required to serve it.

## Rebalancing improvements

- Candidate selection ranks expected incremental value and channel capex rather
  than imbalance alone.
- Positive-EV payer-refill opportunities can receive a bounded budget; known
  negative-EV opportunities remain unexecuted.
- Fresh-flow response, depleted-earner renewal, cooldown clearing, and pair
  futility handling were tightened.
- Native execution can try a safer alternate route and then a bounded amount
  ladder after attributable liquidity failure.
- Actual settled cost, delivered liquidity, post-refill demand, and subsequent
  route profit are linked in the tournament evidence instead of treating a
  successful rebalance as an end in itself.

There is still no Sling dependency. The production path is Revenue Ops policy,
askrene v3 route discovery, native `sendpay`/`waitsendpay`, and restart-safe
local accounting.

## Evidence and observability

- A canonical forward archive preserves bounded, independently reconciled
  forward evidence across CLN retention and pruning boundaries.
- Fee and rebalance cycle capture/replay can reproduce offline decisions using
  sealed, versioned envelopes. Capture is observational and disabled by
  default.
- Daily validation collects read-only fee, profitability, budget,
  reconciliation, and forward evidence with explicit completeness and
  degraded-data classifications.
- Tournament evidence uses fresh contender identities, deterministic mixed
  CLN/LND traffic, identity crossing, exact volume attribution, explicit
  exclusions, spend caps, and safety gates.

## CLBOSS tournament result

The formal frozen series used three fresh crossed replicas and six scored
blocks per replica in both fee-only and full-stack leagues. Revenue Ops won
both leagues; payment settlement was 100%, fallback use was zero, every common
coverage/budget/safety gate passed, and both paired 95% confidence intervals
were entirely above zero.

The final fixed-revision capacity matrix then crossed controller identities at
2M, 5M, and 20M requested channel capacities:

- 768 of 768 payments settled;
- zero fallback settlements;
- zero multipart attribution contamination in accepted blocks;
- zero safety violations;
- Revenue Ops net routing profit: 19,230,613 msat;
- CLBOSS net routing profit: 238,726 msat;
- Revenue Ops routed volume: 26,597,197,870 msat;
- CLBOSS routed volume: 4,271,876,623 msat;
- Revenue Ops rebalance cost: 3,334 msat; and
- CLBOSS rebalance cost: 1,000,800 msat.

Revenue Ops won net profit and routed volume in every accepted final replica
and won aggregate worst-channel balance. These are controlled fake-sat Polar
results, not a promise of identical mainnet performance. The reproducible
[living scorecard](../optimization/CLBOSS_TOURNAMENT_SCORECARD.md) records the
formal verdict, broader diagnostics, and exclusions.

## Upgrade from v2.19.0

1. Upgrade Core Lightning to a maintainer-verified v26.06.7 or newer release
   artifact. Do not rely only on a version string or the initially published
   v26.06.7 container tag; see the
   [compatibility record](../CORE_LIGHTNING_COMPATIBILITY.md).
2. Confirm all old planner, Boltz, LN+, open/close, and expansion-treasury
   executors are inactive and no old operation is unresolved.
3. Back up the Revenue Ops SQLite database and current config.
4. Use `tools/liquidity_decommission_preflight.py` and
   `tools/render_liquidity_decommission_config.py` to remove retired v2
   options while preserving a safe rollback config.
5. Stop only the plugin, fast-forward the checkout to this tag, and start the
   resolved `cl-revenue-ops.py` entrypoint dynamically.
6. Read back `plugin list`, `revenue-status`, `revenue-health`,
   `revenue-fee-debug`, `revenue-rebalance-debug`, budgets, and profitability.
7. Confirm removed RPC families and executor threads are absent. Do not invoke
   removed action RPCs merely to test their absence.

Runtime `revenue-config` database overrides take precedence over file and
`setconfig` values. Review or reset old overrides deliberately rather than
assuming a new config file supersedes them.
