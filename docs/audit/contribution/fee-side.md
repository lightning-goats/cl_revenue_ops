# Phase 4 — Fee-Side Contribution Analysis (2026-07-01 frozen corpus)

Tests the **pre-registered** fee-side hypotheses (fee_controller.md §5 FC-H1..H3;
flow_analysis.md, policy_manager.md, hive_hints.md §5) against the frozen hermes
corpus, and builds the revenue decomposition that anchors the campaign. Every
number here is regenerated by running
`tools/audit/contrib_fee_analysis.py` (read-only, deterministic — two full runs
reproduced byte-identically; seeded bootstrap n=10,000). Registered definitions
were applied as written; where a hypothesis is untestable as registered, that is
the verdict and any further analysis is explicitly labelled exploratory.

**Multiple comparisons:** five confirmatory tests were actually runnable
(FC-H1, FC-H2a, FA-H1, FA-H2a-n1, FA-H3-n1). Holm-Bonferroni-adjusted p-values
are reported in §10. Only FC-H2a has raw p < 0.05, and it does **not** survive
correction (holm-adj 0.068) — nor, more importantly, does its causal
attribution survive the deploy-confound analysis (§3).

## 1. Revenue decomposition (anchor)

Phase 2/3 documents quote the nexus-01 chain as 17,475 deduplicated forwards /
1,033 settled / 20,329.884 sats. Re-running the repo's own
`check_hermes_forwards_chain.py` against the frozen corpus gives
**36,204 deduplicated forwards / 1,732 settled / 28,530.450 sats**
(updated_index 79,824..116,027, 2026-05-20T17:28→2026-07-01T19:51, contiguous,

(18,729 forwards, 699 settled, 8,200.566 sats, start=97,299) which covers the

## 2. FC-H1 — DTS repricing of stagnant channels vs holding

**Registered:** population = channels with ≥3 consecutive zero-revenue days
whose fee the controller then lowered (`dts_pid_sample`, delta<0); control =
matched channel (same node, similar capacity decile, prior 7-day revenue) whose
fee did not change in the same window; outcomes = fees/day and
forwards-resumption over the 7 days after; matched/paired bootstrap, 95% CI
excluding zero.

**Implementation choices (declared):** zero-revenue days are full UTC days with
zero out-attributed settled fees; one event per channel per 7 days (first
kept); the external-writer channel 946890x2272x0 excluded from treatment and
control (its fee path is not controller policy); control fee-constancy verified
on the hourly advertised-fee (listpeerchannels) series, which is robust to
record loss; matching = same/adjacent capacity decile, then nearest prior-7d
revenue.

**Result: INCONCLUSIVE.** n = 17 matched pairs (all nexus-01; nexus-02
produced 2 candidate events with no eligible control, and every n2 outcome is a
hard zero anyway):

- fees/day, 7 days after: treated mean 1.237 vs control 0.414 sats/day; paired
  difference **+0.823 sats/day [95% CI −0.828, +2.770]** — CI includes zero.
  Median difference 0.000 (12/17 pairs are 0-vs-0). Exact sign test on the 5
  non-zero pairs: 4/5 favor treatment, p = 0.375.
- resumption within 7d: treated 5/17 (29%) vs control 1/17 (6%); McNemar exact
  on discordant pairs (5 vs 1) p = 0.219.

Direction consistently favors repricing (the three biggest earners after
treatment — 953555x1338x0 +13.0, 944584x1998x0 +6.5, 950402x2686x0 +1.5
sats/day — were all fee-drops into the 60-ppm floor region), but the corpus
does not have the power to confirm it.

**Record-loss limit (binding):** treatments were identified from recovered
records only — nexus-01 recovery is **3,610 of 9,152 ids (60.6% lost)** across
367 gaps (nexus-02: 455/456). Bounding via LPC: **13 additional
LPC-visible unrecorded fee-drops on stagnant channels** (deduplicated
one-per-channel-per-7d) are probable treatment events lost to the 10-row RPC
echo — i.e., the identified treatment set (17) captured only ~57% of candidate
events. Because control status is LPC-verified (loss-robust), record loss can
shrink and bias the *treatment* sample but cannot contaminate controls; the
direction of selection bias is unknowable, so the inconclusive verdict stands
with n understated, not overstated.

**Confounders:** treatment events cluster on 06-16 — the zero-flow-ratchet
fleet unwind (deployed 06-15T19:22), so "DTS lowered the fee" in this window is
mostly "the new ratchet lowered the fee"; the effect estimate is therefore
closer to a test of the *post-ratchet* repricing policy than of pre-ratchet DTS
behaviour. The 06-13 mass close changed the channel set immediately before the
cluster; outcome windows extending past 06-20 have control-constancy verified
only through the last LPC snapshot (all 17 windows end after it; sensitivity
row identical because forwards outcomes come from the lossless chain).

## 3. FC-H2 — climb governor (9f8f219, first stamped 2026-06-12T13:13:43Z)

**Registered:** per channel-week (a) overshoot ratio = max advertised fee /
earning-weighted p90 fee (fees present during settled forwards) — decreases;
(b) fees earned — does not decrease; before vs after 2026-06-12; Mann-Whitney U
p<0.05 on (a); bootstrap CI on (b) whose lower bound excludes a >20% decline.

**Coverage constraint (stated up front):** advertised fees exist only from
06-09, so the before-side of (a) is one partial week (wk−1, 3.1 days of LPC);
after-side weeks wk+0 (full) and wk+1 (1.5 days of LPC before the hole).
Shorter coverage biases observed max-fee *down*, which works against the
after-period — i.e., against H2 for wk−1 and for it in wk+1; both directions
are present, so the test is reported as registered with this caveat. (b) uses
full chain-covered weeks only: 3 before, 2 after.

**Result (a):** channel-weeks with defined overshoot: 11 before, 23 after.
Median overshoot **2.81 [1.68, 6.33] before → 1.12 [1.00, 2.71] after**;
one-sided MWU p = 0.0136 (**holm-adjusted 0.068** across the confirmatory
family — does not survive correction).

**Attribution check (decisive):** splitting the after-period at the 071a5b3
zero-flow-ratchet deploy (06-15T19:22):

| epoch | n | median overshoot | mean |
|---|---|---|---|
| pre-governor (≤06-12) | 11 | 2.81 | 5.15 |
| governor→ratchet (06-12→06-15, mixed weeks) | 10 | **3.20** | 5.49 |
| post-ratchet (≥06-15) | 13 | **1.00** | 1.17 |

Between the governor and the ratchet, overshoot did **not** fall — the two
biggest after-period overshoots are new ladders *under the governor*
(946890x2272x0 wk+0 climbed to 2,306 = overshoot 9.22; 944584x1998x0 wk+0
overshoot 18.53; 946628x754x0 8.46). The collapse to ~1.0 coincides with the
ratchet, exactly as Phase 3's guard-trajectory evidence (LF-8: 244/246
correct-direction guard changes, the 720→61 fleet unwind) predicts.

**Result (b):** mean fees/channel-week before 183.27 (n=89) vs after 75.11
(n=102): change **−59.0% [95% CI −90.6%, +57.1%]** — the CI does not exclude a
>20% decline (nor any effect from −90% to +57%). Survivors-only (mass-close
control): −53.3% [−90.4%, +81.9%]. The before-window contains the 06-02..06-08
whale week; with revenue this episodic and concentrated, two weeks of
after-data cannot resolve the earnings guardrail either way.

**Verdict: INCONCLUSIVE as registered.** Overshoot after 06-12 is lower than
before (raw p<0.05, fails Holm), but the registered causal reading — that the
*06-12 climb governor* reduced it — is contradicted by the within-after-period
timing: the reduction arrives with the 06-15 ratchet. The earnings-preservation
arm cannot be resolved. Descriptively, **071a5b3 is the change that ended
optimizer overshoot** (post-ratchet median exactly 1.00); the 06-27 fee-ratchet
fix (441b8e3 era) sits in the corpus hole and only its 07-01 endpoint is
observable.

## 4. FC-H3 — rebalance-floored channels net-positive

**Registered activation:** ≥4 realized rebalance cost samples in 30 days (spend
ledger) on a non-sink/dormant channel with cost_ppm×1.20 above the base floor.

**Census:** all 1,227 spend-ledger snapshots (613 n1 + 614 n2) contain **zero
non-zero spend/cost fields**; dashboard 30d `rebalance_cost_sats` = 0 on both
nodes; `lifetime_rebalance_costs_sats` is frozen at 665 (n1) / 0 (n2) across
the whole corpus — **in-corpus realized rebalance spend is exactly zero**. The
67 n1 fee-change records carrying a `rebal_cost_floor:` tag are the soft-nudge
annotation from pre-corpus cost history, not hard-floor activation (the
registered definition anticipated this distinction).

**Verdict: UNTESTABLE AS REGISTERED** — the activation population is empty.
Nothing exploratory can substitute: with zero spend, "fees minus rebalance
spend" degenerates to §1's decomposition. STOP.

## 5. EXPLORATORY (non-confirmatory) — the external-hand elasticity episode on 946890x2272x0

This channel is 56.0% of all node revenue (15,985 sats, 463 settled), so its
pricing is the single most consequential fee decision in the corpus. Implied
prices of everything it ever earned: 11,596.9 sats below 150 ppm, 4,201.3 at
150–300, **186.7 above 300 ppm** (9 forwards, lifetime).

**Price-exposure vs revenue, 06-09..06-20** (exposure from the LPC-backbone
advertised-fee timeline, 118 segments, change-instant at diff-interval
midpoint, max interval 1.3 h; revenue priced per forward by exact implied ppm):

| advertised band | exposure | revenue | rate |
|---|---|---|---|
| [0,150) ppm | 88.0 h | 2,184.7 sats (67 fwds) | **24.83 sats/h** |
| [150,300) ppm | 129.9 h | 1,226.9 sats (43 fwds) | 9.44 sats/h |
| [300,1000) ppm | 6.1 h | 0 | 0.00 |
| [1000,∞) ppm | 63.5 h | 0 | **0.00 sats/h** |

**69.6 hours of DTS-ladder exposure above 300 ppm bought exactly zero
forwards.** The demand cutoff is hard, somewhere in 250–300 ppm.

**Held-price contrast around the 06-15 external reset (2306→250):** the
LPC-confirmed held-at-2306 span (06-15T02:53→18:16, 15.4 h) earned **0.0
sats**; the first same-length span held at 250 earned 45.8 sats; the full held
week at 250 (06-15T19:35→06-23) earned **2,862.4 sats = 16.60 sats/h**.
Rest-of-node demand was flat across the boundary (0.83 vs 1.00 sats/h), so
this is not a network-wide demand swing. Counterfactual at DTS's own stale
belief (2,306 ppm, posterior mean 2,257 still visible in the 07-01 records):
~0 sats/h × 168 h ≈ 0 — the external hand's single write is worth roughly the
whole 2.9k-sat week, and the hole period (fee unobservable; 65–66 ppm by the
07-01 records) added another **4,890.8 sats in 10 days**.

**New data-quality finding (E2c):** at 06-15T18:33 — while LPC still showed
2,306 (18:16) — ten MPP shards settled at **exactly 150 ppm implied**
(391.8 sats total). Either a second interim external write (2306→~150→250)
invisible at LPC granularity, or grace-period pricing; either way the "held at
2306" span must end at 18:16, and any analysis pricing forwards by hourly
advertised fee alone would have booked 391.8 sats to a 2,306-ppm regime it
never belonged to. (This analysis prices by implied ppm, which is immune.)

All of §5 is exploratory: treatment (the external write) was not randomized,
n(held-price episodes)=1 per price, and the actor/motive are unrecorded.
It is nevertheless the cleanest price-response evidence in the corpus, and it
is *consistent with* FC-H1's positive-but-unconfirmed direction and with the
fee-band decomposition (§1.1): this node earns below ~250 ppm or not at all.

## 8. FA hypotheses (flow analysis)

### FA-H1 — labels predictive of 24h balance movement: NOT SUPPORTED (direction reversed)

Registered: SOURCE-labelled channel-hours' outbound ratio (spendable/total,
hourly LPC) declines more over the following 24 h than BALANCED-labelled ones;
MWU p<0.05 plus median-difference bootstrap CI excluding zero.

Result on 2,486 labelled channel-hours (518 SOURCE / 1,968 BALANCED, labels
from revenue-status channel_states within 10 min of each LPC point): median
24h Δ **+0.0735 for SOURCE vs +0.0000 for BALANCED** — SOURCE channels'
outbound ratio *rose*. Median difference +0.0735 [95% CI +0.0011, +0.0813]
excludes zero **in the direction opposite to registration**; the registered
one-sided MWU is p≈1.0. Cluster-robust sensitivity (per-channel means, 8 vs 6
channels — channel-hours are heavily autocorrelated) is inconclusive
(p=0.63), so the honest summary is: the registered test refutes the
registered direction; the effect is carried by few channels. Named
confounders: SOURCE hours are dominated by 936056x1037x0 (180 h),
931199x1231x0 (138 h — the node's main inbound feeder, which *fills* while
labelled source), and 946890x2272x0 (111 h — label frozen while the channel
was unmanaged, E2b); the post-ratchet repricing refilled drained channels
during exactly this window. A SOURCE label in this corpus tracked *recent
outflow history*, not *future net drain*.

### FA-H2 — hysteresis reduced churn without hurting earnings: NOT SUPPORTED (premise absent)

Registered baseline is "corpus days before the F1 deployment … the audited
~1.3 flips/day", with target ≤0.3. In-corpus, F1 (2df8d92) first appears
06-12T00:41:52Z, leaving a 2.99-day baseline: churn was already
**0.115 flips/channel-day before** F1 and 0.126 after (n1; rate-ratio p=0.68;
n2 zero flips in both eras — vacuous). Both eras sit far below both the ~1.3
premise and the ≤0.3 target: whatever fixed label churn happened before this
corpus begins, and the corpus cannot attribute it to F1. Earnings guardrail
(b): node fees/day −17.1% [−53.4%, +60.9%] — cannot exclude a >20% decline,
but the before-window is whale-week-loaded (§1.1) and network-shift
confounded; no adverse earnings effect is *demonstrated* either.

### FA-H3 — depletion forecasts calibrated enough to act on: REFUTED under the registered replay

Registered derivation: no kalman artifact exists in the corpus, so filter
state must be replay-derived — re-run the module's Kalman over the forwards
chain plus listpeerchannels spendable/capacity, discard ≥5 burn-in days, then:
among channel-hours where `estimate_depletion_hours` predicts depletion ≤24 h,
the fraction whose outbound ratio actually falls below 0.1 within 36 h must
exceed ≥3× the base rate among no-prediction channel-hours.

Replay fidelity: the filter arithmetic is the module's own code
(`modules.flow_analysis.KalmanFlowFilter`, imported, not re-implemented), fed
at the production flow-analysis cadence (flow-interval=3600 s) with the
module's observation pipeline reconstructed exactly (24 h rolling net-flow
ratio per `_compute_raw_kalman_observation` with the DB's sign convention;
confidence per `_calculate_confidence`; volatility from 7-day daily buckets
per `_calculate_kalman_volatility`; predict-only when no observation;
convergence gates `KALMAN_MIN_OBSERVATIONS`/uncertainty enforced). Burn-in:
replay starts 05-20, evaluation starts 06-09 (≥19 days). Channel-hours already
below 0.1 outbound at prediction time are excluded (103).

Result (n1): **678 positive-prediction channel-hours (6 deduplicated
episodes) → 0 hit**; 14,981 negative channel-hours → 150 hit (base rate
1.00%). Ratio 0.0× against a ≥3× bar; Fisher one-sided p=1.0. The forecasts
were not merely uninformative — the flagged group did *worse* than base rate.
Mechanism visible in the episodes (e.g. 946890x2272x0 on 06-09,
931199x1231x0 on 06-18): high recent drain triggers the forecast, then fees
and demand dynamics throttle the drain before spendable reaches 10%. n2:
vacuous (no forwards → no drain observations → no predictions).

Caveats: replay-derived states are not production states (production filter
history began before 05-20; its persisted state at corpus start is unknown),
and the 36 h outcome lookahead requires LPC coverage (evaluation windows with
<30 h of lookahead were dropped). The refutation is therefore of "the
registered replay method finds calibration", which is precisely what was
registered.

## 9. Cross-cutting evidence limits

- Snapshot surfaces: ~12 observed days + one 07-01 capture; 10-day hole. Only
  the forwards chain (and therefore all *outcome* legs) covers the hole;
  advertised fees, labels, and hints are unobservable inside it.
- nexus-01 fee-change record recovery 39.4% (5,542 of 9,152 ids lost to the
  10-row RPC echo; 367 gaps). Quantified impact per section; worst on FC-H1
  treatment identification (§2). nexus-02 is near-complete (455/456) but
  routed nothing, so its exactness buys no outcome power.
- One channel = 56% of revenue; one pair ≈ 80%. Every fee-side aggregate is
  effectively a story about 946890x2272x0 plus a long tail; no test here
  generalizes beyond this node-pair without new data.
- Deploy epochs come from collector-host code stamps (corroborated by guard-
  change timing for the ratchet); treated as accurate to minutes, not seconds.
- The 06-13 operator mass close changed the channel set mid-corpus; survivor
  sensitivity analyses included where it binds (FC-H2b).

## 10. Multiple-comparison summary (Holm-Bonferroni, confirmatory family)

| test | raw p | holm-adj |
|---|---|---|
| FC-H2a overshoot decreases (MWU) | 0.0136 | 0.068 |
| FC-H1 fees/day paired diff (exact sign test) | 0.375 | 1 |
| FA-H2a flips fall (n1) | 0.679 | 1 |
| FA-H1 SOURCE declines more (MWU) | ~1 | 1 |
| FA-H3 depletion calibration (Fisher) | ~1 | 1 |

Nothing in the confirmatory family survives correction. The two robust
positive results of this phase are non-hypothesis artifacts: the revenue
decomposition (§1) and the exploratory elasticity episode (§5), which together
say: this node's demand lives below ~250 ppm, is extremely concentrated, and
the fee stack's measurable contribution in-window was made by the 06-15
zero-flow ratchet (and an external operator hand), not by the DTS optimizer's
price discovery.
